My Ultralearning Project Go + Backend

September 27, 2026

I know, I know…#

I already have a post where I talked about a Go roadmap, but I fell into a rabbit hole of learning.

AND I FOUND THE ULTRALEARNING BOOK.

I really fell in love with the idea and decided to embark on this journey on my own. That also meant changing my “roadmap”, because it is more of a project now and about learning a bunch of practices & methods of an “Ultralearner”. So basically, how to be really intense in short amounts of time, and thus get really impressive results.

If you wanna learn more about the “Ultralearning” approach, I would highly recommend checking out the book. I found it to be a delightful read.

Quick Overview#

Since I randomly decided to make a YT video and blog post, this will be a quick overview of the project. If you decide to follow my journey on X, LinkedIn, and/or YouTube, maybe you can pick up a thing or two ;). I will post every Sunday on YouTube, X, and LinkedIn!

Why? Why Go and Backend?#

  • I wanna build cool projects
  • I like the Gopher
  • Want to have a language where I can build my first real expertise!
  • Backend, because I found it to be a common use case for Go and the entry barrier is relatively “easy” (I hate frontend).

What? What are you gonna learn?#

This one is really simple due to a great resource:

  • Roadmap.sh
  • I will follow the Go & Backend roadmaps
  • Obviously not every single path or option, but rather the “usual” or “typical” stack

How? What are my Methods?#

I will again point mainly to the Ultralearning Book. Nonetheless, here is my approach regarding this specific project:

  • Direct Practice

    • This means doing the actual thing: Coding
    • If I identify any weaknesses, I will use drills to improve those weak areas (more details in the upcoming weeks)

  • Books & Exercises

    • I will read books to understand the concepts
    • Also use book exercises to practice these concepts specifically (compared to direct practice, maybe around 20% of the time)
